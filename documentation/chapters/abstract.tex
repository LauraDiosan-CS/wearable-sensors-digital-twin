\begin{center}
\large\textbf{Abstract}
\end{center}

\noindent Wearables record a single-lead ECG continuously, but the signal is noisy and differs from person to person. We propose a simple cardiac digital twin: a model of the patient's heartbeat, fitted to their own normal beats and shared by five AI agents that clean the signal, detect arrhythmias, forecast heart rate, update the twin and write a report for a clinician (Figure~\ref{fig:graphicalAbstract}). Beats that differ strongly from the twin are flagged as unusual for that patient. We evaluate the agents on public PhysioNet ECG data, always on patients the models have not seen. Adding the twin's deviation score to a beat classifier raises the F1 score for ventricular beats from 0.88 to 0.96.
