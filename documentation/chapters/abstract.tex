\begin{center}
\large\textbf{Abstract}
\end{center}

\noindent Wearables record a single-lead ECG continuously, but the signal is noisy and differs from person to person. We propose a simple cardiac digital twin: a model of the patient's heartbeat, fitted to their own normal beats and shared by five AI agents that clean the signal, detect arrhythmias, forecast heart rate, update the twin and write a report for a clinician. Beats that differ strongly from the twin are flagged as unusual for that patient. This report describes the problem (graphical abstract in Figure~\ref{fig:graphicalAbstract}) and surveys related work. Experiments will use public PhysioNet ECG data.
